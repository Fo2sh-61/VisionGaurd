THE ONE IDEA
It should feel like asking a knowledgeable person a question — not reading a control panel.
A small number of tools. Each answers one question clearly. Everything else stays out of the way until it's asked for.
HOME
One box. Ask in plain words.
“Who was absent today?” routes straight to attendance — no menu hunting.
Needs-your-attention is capped at three real items, never an alert feed.
Tools sit below, quiet until wanted.

Learn one and you have learned all of them.

Two roles, one console
Admin
School staff
Anything involving people: contacting parents, exporting records, managing who can sign in. Their home screen opens on attendance and incidents.
IT
Runs the system
Cameras, recorders, backups, system status. No student records, no account changes. Their home screen opens on what is broken.
Both roles can search for a named person — IT has to be able to check the feature works. Every one of those searches is logged either way.
